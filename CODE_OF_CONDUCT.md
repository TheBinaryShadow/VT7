# VT7 Code of Conduct

VT7 exists to make life better for people who still care about Windows 7. That
goal starts with how we treat one another.

## Our standard

Project spaces should be welcoming, patient, and useful. We expect participants
to:

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect different skill levels, languages, hardware, and reasons for using
  Windows 7.
- Give and receive technical criticism in good faith.
- Keep issue and pull request discussions relevant.
- Credit the work of others.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope while remaining free to
  disagree respectfully.

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments.
- Personal insults or sustained hostility.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately disrupting project work or support discussions.
- Misrepresenting another person's work as your own.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions
should consider context, severity, previous behavior, and the safety of the
people involved.

For an ordinary disagreement, step away if needed and return to the technical
facts. For serious or sensitive conduct concerns, use the project's
[private vulnerability reporting channel](https://github.com/TheBinaryShadow/VT7/security/advisories/new)
and state that the report concerns conduct rather than a software vulnerability.
Do not expose private or sensitive details in a public issue.

Retaliation against someone who reports a good-faith concern is not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests,
release discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing VT7.
